Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping Opens Wide, Tracking a $90–120 Million Debut

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

Panem returns today. The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping opens wide on Friday, 20 November 2026, on about 3,900 screens in the US, including IMAX — and at 158 minutes it is the longest film in the franchise. Presales are running ahead of both Wicked films, and Deadline projects a $90–120 million domestic opening weekend.

Release Wide today, 20 November 2026
Screens About 3,900, including IMAX
Runtime 158 minutes — longest in the franchise
Opening Projection $90–120 million domestic (Deadline)

If the top end of that projection holds, it would be the first $100 million-plus Lionsgate opener since 2015 — a decade-long drought for the studio. The early-access screenings earlier this week and the presale pace both point the same way: this is not just franchise fans showing up, it is a genuine event opening.

The film tells the story of the 50th Hunger Games — Haymitch Abernathy's Games — and the first reviews are split, which only adds to the opening-weekend conversation. The question now is whether the weekend numbers land at the $90 million floor or push past $120 million.

Source: Deadline via Movie Opinions (19 November 2026 check)
