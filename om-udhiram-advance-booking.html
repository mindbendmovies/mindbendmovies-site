OM Chapter 1: Udhiram Advance Booking Smashes Dhanush Records

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

The advance booking numbers are in, and they are big. OM Chapter 1: Udhiram — the action thriller pairing Dhanush with Malayalam legend Mammootty, directed by Rajkumar Periasamy — has sold ₹7.32 crore worth of tickets for Day 1 in advance booking alone, across 5,006 shows and more than 4.09 lakh tickets.

Day 1 Advance ₹7.32 crore gross (₹6.61 crore excluding blocked seats)
Shows / Tickets 5,006 shows • 4.09 lakh tickets sold
Record Dhanush's biggest-ever advance, beating Raayan (₹6.15 crore)
Tamil Share About 75% of presales from the Tamil version
Opening Estimate ₹17–20 crore India • ₹25–30 crore worldwide (Sacnilk)

For Dhanush, this is a career-best advance — comfortably ahead of Raayan's ₹6.15 crore — and it signals how strongly the Dhanush–Mammootty pairing has cut across markets. With roughly three-quarters of the presales coming from the Tamil version and strong pickup elsewhere, trade trackers are now pencilling in a ₹17–20 crore opening day in India.

Worldwide, the opening day is estimated at ₹25–30 crore. If the film lands at the top of that range and word of mouth holds, Udhiram could set the tone for the rest of the year's Tamil box office — and give both its stars one of their biggest openers ever.

Source: The Hollywood Reporter India • Sacnilk
