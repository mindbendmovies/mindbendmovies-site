White Blast Tops Korean Box Office for Second Straight Day

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

White Blast has held the No. 1 spot at the Korean box office for a second straight day. The film drew 48,609 admissions on 19 November, taking its cumulative total to 112,306 — a strong hold after topping the chart on its opening day.

Admissions (19 Nov) 48,609
Cumulative Admissions 112,306
No. 2 Fall 2: Deadpoint — 12,240 admissions
No. 3 The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping — 11,000 admissions

The gap behind it is wide: Fall 2: Deadpoint sits second with 12,240 admissions, while The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping — which opens wide in the US today — is third in Korea with 11,000 admissions. White Blast is pulling roughly four times the daily audience of its nearest rival.

Yesterday's chart told the same story at the start: White Blast opened at No. 1, and day two confirms it was not a one-day spike. If the hold continues through the weekend, it will be the Korean box office story of the week.

Source: Yonhap via K-VIBE • SBS Star
