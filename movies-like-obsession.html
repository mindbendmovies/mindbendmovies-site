15 Movies Like Obsession You Can Stream Tonight

Movies Like · Psychological Thrillers · Obsession & Stalker Films
Updated November 21, 2026 · US streaming checked

Obsession — the Netflix horror hit about a man who snaps a One Wish Willow stick so his crush will love him, and gets far more love than any human can survive — leaves a very specific itch behind: love as a curse, devotion as a threat. If that ending is still living in your head, start with our Obsession ending explained breakdown, then work through this list.

Every pick below earns its place the same way: somebody wants somebody else too much, and the wanting turns dangerous. Classics, cult films and modern Netflix-era thrillers — all fifteen are in our library with full movie pages, trailers and US where-to-watch.

Short on time? The three closest matches are Fatal Attraction (the original), Misery (devotion as captivity) and Greta (on Netflix right now, like Obsession itself).

1. Fatal Attraction

1987 · ★ 6.8

The blueprint for the whole genre. A married man's brief affair turns into a nightmare when the other woman simply refuses to be discarded — the original love-that-won't-let-go thriller that Obsession descends from.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Paramount+, fuboTV

2. Misery

1990 · ★ 7.8

A superfan rescues her favourite author after a crash — then holds him captive until he writes the ending she wants. Devotion as a prison: the purest, scariest form of the fantasy behind Bear's wish.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): AMC

3. Gone Girl

2014 · ★ 7.9

A wife stages her own disappearance and rewrites her marriage from the shadows. If the wish-controlled devotion in Obsession chilled you, Amy Dunne's calculated version will too.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Paramount+

4. Audition

2000 · ★ 7.1

A widower holds fake auditions to find a new wife and chooses the quiet one — who has been quietly choosing him apart. Slow, polite, and then suddenly one of the most feared final acts in horror.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): ARROW, Shudder channels, AsianCrush

5. Possession

1981 · ★ 7.3

A marriage rots into something inhuman inside a Berlin apartment. The most extreme film on this list: obsession that doesn't just control a person, it transforms them.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Shudder, Criterion Channel, Philo

6. Basic Instinct

1992 · ★ 7.0

A detective investigates a murder and falls for the prime suspect — a writer who may be scripting him into her next book. Desire and danger in the exact same person.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Paramount+, fuboTV, MGM+

7. The Crush

1993 · ★ 6.7

A teenage girl fixes on the older writer renting her parents' guest house, and rejection turns her fixation into sabotage. A dark mirror of Nikki's wish-born certainty that someone belongs to her.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Amazon Prime Video

8. Fear

1996 · ★ 6.7

A charming boyfriend's possessive streak escalates from jealousy to a full home-invasion finale. The 90s teen-thriller version of love curdling into control.

Where to watch: Check the movie page for current US streaming and rental options

9. Greta

2019 · ★ 6.5

A young woman returns a lost handbag and gains a lonely older friend whose affection quietly becomes a trap. Bonus for Obsession fans: it's streaming on Netflix too.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Netflix

10. The Gift

2015 · ★ 6.7

An old acquaintance drifts back into a couple's life bearing gifts that feel more like warnings. Obsession stretched over years of guilt — and it does not knock twice.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Criterion Channel, Lionsgate+

11. One Hour Photo

2002 · ★ 6.7

A photo-lab clerk builds an imaginary life inside one family's snapshots. The quietest film here, and one of the saddest takes on wanting to belong to somebody.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Criterion Channel

12. Notes on a Scandal

2006 · ★ 7.1

A lonely teacher discovers her young colleague's secret — and uses it to bind her closer. Friendship as leverage: obsession wearing a polite, respectable face.

Where to watch: Check the movie page for current US streaming and rental options

13. The Hand That Rocks the Cradle

1992 · ★ 6.9

A nanny with a hidden grudge moves into the home of the family she blames for her loss. The threat isn't outside the house — she's the one tucking the children in.

Where to watch: Check the movie page for current US streaming and rental options

14. Sleeping with the Enemy

1991 · ★ 6.5

A wife fakes her own death to escape a controlling husband who refuses to accept that she is gone. Possession disguised as a marriage — and he is very good at finding people.

Where to watch: Check the movie page for current US streaming and rental options

15. Side Effects

2013 · ★ 6.6

A psychiatrist, a depressed patient, a new pill — and a death that may not be what it seems. Desire and manipulation, finished with a twist that re-reads everything before it.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Amazon Prime Video
