Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle Passes ¥100 Billion Worldwide

Movie News • 19 November 2026
Updated 19 November 2026

The Demon Slayer phenomenon has reached another historic height. Demon Slayer: Infinity Castle has passed ¥100 billion at the worldwide box office — a milestone only a tiny handful of Japanese films have ever achieved.

Milestone ¥100 billion worldwide
Film Infinity Castle — the first of the closing trilogy
Franchise Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

Infinity Castle adapts the manga's final, all-out battle arc and opens a planned trilogy that will close the Demon Slayer story on the big screen. The franchise has form for rewriting record books: its previous theatrical release, Mugen Train, became the highest-grossing Japanese film of all time — and Infinity Castle is now marching into the same territory.

With two more films still to come in the trilogy, the ¥100 billion mark is unlikely to be the ceiling. Anime is no longer a niche at the global box office — Demon Slayer keeps proving it can stand toe-to-toe with the biggest Hollywood franchises.

Source: Worldwide box office reporting (19 November 2026)
