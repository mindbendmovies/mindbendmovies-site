15 Movies Like Collision Course You Can Stream Tonight

Movies Like · Heist Thrillers · Timelines & Betrayal Films
Updated November 21, 2026 · US streaming checked

Collision Course — the Spanish Netflix hit that went No. 1 in 25 countries — rewinds the same getaway crash five times, and every version exposes a different lie: an affair, a stolen stone, a swapped diamond in safe deposit box 34. If that ending is still living in your head, start with our Collision Course ending explained breakdown, then work through this list.

Every pick below earns its place the same way: a crew, a plan, and a betrayal that was already in place before the job began. Heist classics, twist thrillers and films that replay time itself — all fifteen are in our library with full movie pages, trailers and US where-to-watch.

Short on time? The three closest matches are Run Lola Run (the same event, replayed), Inside Man (a bank job with an inside man) and Reservoir Dogs (a heist undone by a traitor in the crew).

1. Run Lola Run

1998 · ★ 7.3

The closest match on this list: the same twenty minutes replayed three times, each version turning on one small choice — a crash, a delay, a different death. Collision Course does the same trick with a getaway crash and five timelines.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Sundance Now, Sony Pictures Core Amazon Channel

2. Inside Man

2006 · ★ 7.4

A bank robbery where the robbers are already inside the story — a hostage situation with a plan nobody in the building understands until the last scene. The inside-man twist is pure Collision Course.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Philo, Starz Amazon Channel, Starz

3. Reservoir Dogs

1992 · ★ 8.1

A heist that falls apart before the film even starts, then rewinds to show who betrayed whom. A crew of criminals who do not trust each other, told out of order — the DNA of Collision Course's five versions.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Amazon Prime Video, Amazon Prime Video with Ads, MovieSphere+ Amazon Channel

4. Heat

1995 · ★ 7.9

A professional crew, a cop who thinks like them, and a job that should have been clean. The gold standard for heist films where the plan matters less than the people carrying it out.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, YouTube TV

5. The Usual Suspects

1995 · ★ 8.2

Five criminals, one lineup, and a story that keeps changing every time it is retold. If you loved finding out who really planned the Collision Course robbery, this is the twist that invented the game.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): HBO Max Amazon Channel, Cinemax Amazon Channel, HBO Max

6. Ocean's Eleven

2001 · ★ 7.5

The smooth, fun side of the crew heist: eleven specialists, one vault, and a plan with a second plan hiding inside it. Watch it as a palate cleanser after Collision Course's darker timelines.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): HBO Max Amazon Channel, YouTube TV, Cinemax Amazon Channel

7. The Town

2010 · ★ 7.2

A Boston bank crew whose next job is threatened by a witness, a cop closing in, and loyalty cracking inside the group. Heist craft plus the same question Collision Course asks: can you ever really get out?

Where to watch: Streaming (US): HBO Max Amazon Channel, Cinemax Amazon Channel, Cinemax Apple TV channel

8. Den of Thieves

2018 · ★ 6.9

Cops and robbers circling the same Los Angeles bank job, each side convinced the other is the criminal. Gritty, violent, and built around a heist with a twist in the vault.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): fuboTV, AMC+ Roku Premium Channel, YouTube TV

9. Baby Driver

2017 · ★ 7.4

A getaway driver who hears the whole job as music — until one crash turns a clean escape into a chase. The getaway-gone-wrong energy of Collision Course, turned up to eleven.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): fuboTV, Paramount Plus Premium, Paramount+ Amazon Channel

10. Snatch

2000 · ★ 7.8

A stolen diamond the size of a fist, passed from thief to thief while everybody lies about where it is. If the fake diamonds in box 34 made you laugh darkly, this is the film that joke comes from.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Sundance Now

11. Wrath of Man

2021 · ★ 7.6

A quiet new hire on an armoured-truck crew is not who he says he is — and the robbery he is really planning started long before he applied. Hidden motives, told in chapters.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Amazon Prime Video, Amazon Prime Video with Ads

12. Fargo

1996 · ★ 7.8

A small crime — a fake kidnapping for insurance money — that unravels one bad decision at a time. Proof that the best crime films are about ordinary people lying to themselves.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): fuboTV, MGM+ Amazon Channel, Peacock Premium

13. Memento

2000 · ★ 8.2

Told backwards, one fragment at a time, until the 'truth' you trusted at the start falls apart. The same feeling Collision Course gives you when the next timeline rewrites the last one.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Hulu

14. Pulp Fiction

1994 · ★ 8.5

Three crime stories shuffled out of order, where a throwaway detail in one scene becomes the whole point in another. The original lesson in how reordering time changes what a crime means.

Where to watch: Streaming (US): Apple TV, Paramount Plus Premium, Paramount Plus Essential

15. City of God

2002 · ★ 8.4

Two boys, one favela, twenty years of crime told in flashes that jump across time. A different country and a bigger canvas — but the same idea: the betrayal was already in place before the first shot.
