Godzilla Minus Zero Passes $48 Million in North America

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

The King of the Monsters is still standing tall. Godzilla Minus Zero has crossed $48 million at the North American box office, following its franchise-record $30.6 million opening weekend — and the film has already banked more than $100 million worldwide.

North America About $48.1 million and counting
Opening Weekend $30.6 million — a franchise record
Second Weekend About $7.5 million (down roughly 72–75%)
Worldwide More than $100 million

The second weekend brought a steep drop of roughly 72–75%, to about $7.5 million, as new competition arrived — but context matters: the film front-loaded its audience into a record opening, and its worldwide total has already passed $100 million, powered by a huge run in Japan and strong holds in key international markets.

For Toho, the message is clear: a period-set, character-first Godzilla film can open like a blockbuster in North America. Expect the $50 million domestic milestone to fall within days, with the film's final worldwide tally still climbing.

Source: Variety box office reporting
