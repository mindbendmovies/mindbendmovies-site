Se7en (1995)

1995 • Crime • Mystery • Thriller • 2h 7m

A killer is turning the seven deadly sins into murder scenes — gluttony, greed, sloth, envy, wrath, pride and lust — and leaving each victim as a message. Veteran detective William Somerset, days from retirement, is paired with the reckless young David Mills to stop him.

The deeper they follow the clues, the clearer it becomes that the killer is always one step ahead — and that the final two sins are meant for the detectives themselves. David Fincher's rain-soaked nightmare ends with one of the most quoted final scenes in cinema history.
