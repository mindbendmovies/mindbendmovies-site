Chiikawa Movie Nears 3 Million Admissions in Korea

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

One of the year's sweetest box office stories is still going strong. Chiikawa the Movie: The Secret of the Mermaid Island has held the No. 1 spot at the Korean box office for a fifth straight weekend — and it is now on the verge of passing 3 million admissions in Korea alone.

Korean Rank No. 1 for five straight weekends
Nov 13–15 Weekend $875,745 • 116,176 admissions
Cumulative (Korea) 2,949,096 admissions • $22.6 million
Next Milestone 3 million admissions

Over the November 13–15 weekend the film added another $875,745 from 116,176 admissions, taking its Korean cumulative to 2,949,096 admissions and $22.6 million. For a gentle, small-scale Japanese animated film, out-drawing major Hollywood releases week after week is a remarkable feat.

The run underlines just how powerful the Chiikawa phenomenon has become across Asia — what began as a manga and short-form anime about tiny, cute creatures has turned into a genuine theatrical event, and Korean audiences are still turning up five weekends in.

Source: Edaily (16 November 2026)
