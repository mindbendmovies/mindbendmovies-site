I Play Rocky Goes Wide With 87% on Rotten Tomatoes

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

The little biopic that could is about to get its big moment. I Play Rocky — Peter Farrelly's film about the making of Rocky, with Anthony Ippolito playing a young Sylvester Stallone — expands to around 2,000 theaters across North America today, Friday 20 November.

Expansion About 2,000 theaters from Friday, 20 November
Limited Run Averaged $17,024 per theater in limited release
Rotten Tomatoes 87% critics • 98% audiences — the weekend's best scores
Opening Tracking About $12 million
Timing Opens one day before Rocky's 50th anniversary

The expansion follows a limited run that averaged a remarkable $17,024 per theater, and the film arrives carrying the strongest reviews of the weekend: 87% from critics and a near-perfect 98% audience score on Rotten Tomatoes. Tracking points to an opening of around $12 million — a strong result for a period drama with no franchise behind it.

The timing could hardly be better. Tomorrow marks 50 years since Rocky first stepped into theaters in 1976 and became one of the most beloved underdog stories in cinema history. I Play Rocky tells the story behind that story — and audiences are clearly ready to go the distance again.

Source: Cinema United • Rotten Tomatoes
