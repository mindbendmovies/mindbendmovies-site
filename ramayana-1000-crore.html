Ramayana Beats KGF Chapter 2, Crosses ₹1,000 Crore Gross in India

Movie News • 20 November 2026
Updated 20 November 2026

Ramayana has crossed ₹1,000 crore gross in India, and in doing so it has passed a record many thought would stand for years. On Day 15 the film added ₹19.35 crore in India — a dip of 16% from the previous day, but still enough to take its India nett total to ₹863.75 crore, ahead of KGF Chapter 2's lifetime India nett of ₹859.7 crore. Ramayana is now Yash's highest-grossing film ever in India.

India Gross (15 days) ₹1,032.15 crore
Worldwide Gross ₹1,547.15 crore in 15 days
Day 15 Collection ₹19.35 crore (dip 16%)
Day 16 Advance ₹6.97 crore — 2,73,518 tickets, 74% in 3D

The scale of the run is clearest in the worldwide number: ₹1,547.15 crore gross in just 15 days. The film is not slowing down at the advance-booking window either — Day 16 advance sales already stand at ₹6.97 crore across 2,73,518 tickets, with 74% of those bookings in 3D, showing audiences are still choosing the biggest-screen version of the film.

Passing KGF Chapter 2 matters because that film defined the ceiling for a Kannada-led blockbuster in India. Ramayana has now moved that ceiling, and with strong 3D advance bookings still coming in, the next milestones — the all-time India rankings above it — are firmly in sight.

Source: Filmibeat (20 November 2026) • Bollywood Hungama
